Sentence rhythm
Indonesian is syllable-timed: every syllable gets roughly equal length and weight. English is stress-timed and compresses unstressed syllables toward “duh” to keep a beat — that instinct will speed up and swallow parts of this sentence that Indonesian keeps full.
