Tap Start, then put the phone down

How it works. You hear an Indonesian word. Say what it means in English (“friend”, “to eat”) and it grades you. Or grade yourself instead: good, easy, hard, or don’t know. Either way you hear the answer before the next word. If it can’t tell, it reads the answer and asks right or wrong?

Any time: repeat, skip, stop, or go back to return to the word before (its grade is taken back so you can answer it again). Two silences in a row pause it; say resume to carry on.

Keep the screen on. Browsers stop listening when the phone locks, so this page keeps the screen awake. Use Pocket lock to black it out and ignore touches (hold the button on the black screen to unlock). Earbuds help: the mic won’t hear the page talking, and iPhone plays louder through them while it’s listening.
